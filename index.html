Meet the Designer

Adesola Ogunsawe

Founder & Creative Director

Adesola Ogunsawe is a creative and detail-oriented Fashion Designer, Entrepreneur, and Creative Director based in Lagos, Nigeria. Armed with a Bachelor of Science in Banking and Finance from Olabisi Onabanjo University, Adesola blends sharp analytical business acumen with a profound artistic calling to create a luxury brand that stands for absolute quality and sophistication.

Since founding **SOLA by Adesola** in 2018, she has dedicated herself to defining a signature silhouette for the modern woman. Her design philosophy revolves around clean structural lines, elegant fabric selections, and luxury finishes, transforming raw concepts into wearable art.
